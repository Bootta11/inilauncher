Home screen for Android

Less phone.
More you.

Inilauncher is a calm, text-first home screen. A few apps, a fast search and simple gestures. When you want more, turn on only the extras you need.

No ads · No tracking · Internet features are opt‑in

Quiet by default

Just text

App names instead of icons, so your phone stops calling for attention. Icons are there if you want them.

Fast search

Start typing to find any app, contact or setting. Do quick sums with the built-in calculator.

Simple gestures

Swipe left or right to open an app, swipe down for notifications, double tap to lock.

Screen time

See how long you've used your phone today, right on the home screen.

  • Icon packs

  • Locked hidden apps
  • E-ink mode
  • Backup & share your setup

Your phone, your data

Inilauncher has no account, no ads and no analytics. What it knows about you stays on your phone. The few features that use the internet, like weather, are off until you turn them on, and the privacy policy lists exactly what each one sends.
