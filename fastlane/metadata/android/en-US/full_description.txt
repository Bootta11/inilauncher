Inilauncher is a minimal, text-first home screen for Android, based on the open-source Olauncher.

Out of the box it is clean and quiet: a few apps on the home screen, a fast searchable app list and simple gestures. When you want more, the Extras screen adds optional features such as weather, upcoming events, widgets, folders, notifications on apps, contact and calculator search, icons and fonts. Each one is off until you turn it on.

No ads. No analytics. Features that use the internet are opt-in.
